# Lesson 3: an ADSR Envelope through a VCA turns a held tone into notes.
title Envelopes
about Turn a tone that never stops into notes that start and end
patch envelopes.json

say A Note In plays an Oscillator through a VCA. Press **▶ Play**, then play the `Z` to `M` keys on your computer keyboard.
wait play

say The pitch follows the keys, but the tone never stops: nothing tells the VCA when a note ends. Right-click below the VCA and add **Modulation › ADSR Envelope**.
point canvas
show rclick canvas
wait add mod.adsr

say The Note In's **Gate** is on while a key is held. Patch it into the envelope's **Gate**. Green cables carry gates.
point input.poly_midi out:Gate
point mod.adsr in:Gate
show drag input.poly_midi out:Gate to mod.adsr in:Gate
wait connect input.poly_midi Gate -> mod.adsr Gate

say Now patch the envelope's **Out** into the VCA's **CV**, so the envelope sets how loud the sound is.
point mod.adsr out:Out
point util.vca in:CV
show drag mod.adsr out:Out to util.vca in:CV
wait connect mod.adsr Out -> util.vca CV

say Play a few notes. Each one rises as its key goes down and fades when you let go, and the envelope draws the shape as it runs.
wait notes 3

say For a pluck, turn **Sus** right down, below 0.15. Now a note dies away even while you hold its key.
point mod.adsr knob:Sustain
show turn mod.adsr knob:Sustain down
wait param mod.adsr Sustain < 0.15

say Now a swell: turn **Atk** up past half a second, and play a long note to hear it bloom.
point mod.adsr knob:Attack
show turn mod.adsr knob:Attack up
wait param mod.adsr Attack > 0.5

say Last, **Rel**: past one second, and each note lingers after you let it go.
point mod.adsr knob:Release
show turn mod.adsr knob:Release up
wait param mod.adsr Release > 1

say Play a few slow notes and listen to them fade.
wait notes 2

say Attack, decay, sustain, release: four numbers that give a sound its shape in time. Every instrument has one.
say Next, **Your First Sequence** plays the notes for you, in time.
