# Lesson 5: group the voice from Your First Patch, go inside it, come back.
title Groups
about Fold modules into one, go inside, and come back out
patch groups.json

say Here's the voice from **Your First Patch**: a Note In and an Audio Output, with four modules between them. Press **▶ Play**.
wait play

say Play a few notes on the `Z` to `M` keys.
wait notes 2

say Select the four modules in the middle: drag a box around them, starting on empty canvas, or `Ctrl`-click each one. Then press `Ctrl` + `G`, or right-click one of them and choose **Group**.
point osc.sine
point filter.svf
point util.vca
point mod.adsr
show drag osc.sine -36 -36 to mod.adsr 200 240
wait group

say The four are one module now, with a jack for each cable that crosses its edge. Type a name for it and press `Enter`.
say Then play a few notes: it sounds just the same. Groups cost nothing to run.
wait notes 2

say Double-click the group to go inside, or select it and press `Tab`.
point group
show dclick group
wait enter

say Inside are the four modules, with **Inputs** and **Outputs** at the edges, where the cables come in and go out. The trail at the top shows where you are. Press `Escape`, or click the first name in the trail, to come back out.
wait leave

say A group can hold groups, be saved to your library and dropped into another patch. Right-click it to see what else it can do.
say That's all five lessons. The **📚 Examples** menu has whole patches to take apart, and the manual has a page for every module.
