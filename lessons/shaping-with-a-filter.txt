# Lesson 2: a lowpass filter between a saw and the output, and the Cable
# Probe to hear what a cable carries.
title Shaping with a Filter
about Carve a bright saw with a lowpass filter, then hear what a cable carries
patch shaping-with-a-filter.json

say Here's a saw wave, an octave down, going straight out. Press **▶ Play** to hear it.
wait play

say A **filter** takes part of a sound away. Right-click the canvas between the two modules and choose **Filter › SVF Filter**.
point canvas
show rclick canvas
wait add filter.svf

say Send the saw into the filter: drag from the Oscillator's **Out** to the filter's **In**.
point osc.sine out:Out
point filter.svf in:In
show drag osc.sine out:Out to filter.svf in:In
wait connect osc.sine Out -> filter.svf In

say Now take the filter's **LowPass** out to the Audio Output's **Mono**. Each input takes one cable, so this one replaces the cable that's there.
point filter.svf out:LowPass
point output.audio in:Mono
show drag filter.svf out:LowPass to output.audio in:Mono
wait connect filter.svf LowPass -> output.audio Mono

say Drag the **Cutoff** knob down, below 400 Hz. The saw turns dark and muffled as the filter closes over its harmonics, and the display draws what's let through.
point filter.svf knob:Cutoff
show turn filter.svf knob:Cutoff down
wait param filter.svf Cutoff < 400

say Now turn **Res** up past 0.8. The filter rings at the cutoff, with a vocal, nasal edge. Sweep **Cutoff** again to hear it move.
point filter.svf knob:Resonance
show turn filter.svf knob:Resonance up
wait param filter.svf Resonance > 0.8

say Hold `Alt` and rest the pointer on the cable from the Oscillator into the filter. You hear what that cable carries, the bright saw before the filter, and see its wave.
point osc.sine out:Out
point filter.svf in:In
wait probe 1.5

say That's subtractive synthesis: start bright, then carve. Hold `Alt` over any cable, in any patch, to hear it.
say Next, **Envelopes** turns a tone that never stops into notes.
