# Lesson 1: from an empty canvas to a tone you can hear and change.
# The format is in src/app/lesson.rs.
title First Sound
about An oscillator, an output and one cable: a sound from nothing

say Every sound in Soba starts at a **source**. Right-click the empty canvas and choose **Source › Oscillator**.
say Or press `Space`, type `osc` and press `Enter`.
point canvas
show rclick canvas
wait add osc.sine

say A sound needs a way out to your speakers. Right-click the canvas to the right of the Oscillator and choose **Output › Audio Output**.
point canvas
show rclick canvas
wait add output.audio

say Now the cable. Drag from the Oscillator's **Out** jack to the Audio Output's **Mono** jack. Blue cables carry sound.
point osc.sine out:Out
point output.audio in:Mono
show drag osc.sine out:Out to output.audio in:Mono
wait connect osc.sine Out -> output.audio Mono

say Press **▶ Play** in the toolbar.
wait play

say That steady tone is a sine wave at middle C, and the Oscillator draws it as it plays. Listen for a moment.
point osc.sine out:Out
wait listen 3

say A sine is the purest tone there is. Open the Oscillator's **Wave** menu and choose **Saw**.
point osc.sine knob:Waveform
show click osc.sine knob:Waveform
wait param osc.sine Waveform = Saw

say Brighter and buzzier: a saw holds every harmonic at once. Now drag the **Oct** knob up or down to move it an octave.
point osc.sine knob:Octave
show turn osc.sine knob:Octave up
wait param osc.sine Octave changed

say That's a patch: a source, a way out and a cable between them. Every patch you build grows from this one.
say Next, **Shaping with a Filter** carves a bright saw into something darker.
