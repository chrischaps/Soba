# Lesson 4: a Clock steps a Step Sequencer, which plays a voice.
title Your First Sequence
about A Clock and a Step Sequencer play a looping bass line
patch your-first-sequence.json

say Here's a voice, an Oscillator, a VCA and an envelope, with nothing to play it. A **Clock** keeps time: right-click the empty canvas on the left and add **Utility › Clock**.
point canvas
show rclick canvas
wait add util.clock

say A **Step Sequencer** plays a row of notes, one step for each tick. Add **Utility › Step Sequencer** below the Clock.
point canvas
show rclick canvas
wait add seq.step

say Patch the Clock's **Gate** into the sequencer's **Clock**. Each tick moves it on a step.
point util.clock out:Gate
point seq.step in:Clock
show drag util.clock out:Gate to seq.step in:Clock
wait connect util.clock Gate -> seq.step Clock

say The sequencer's **Pitch** sets the note. Patch it into the Oscillator's **V/Oct**.
point seq.step out:Pitch
point osc.sine in:V/Oct
show drag seq.step out:Pitch to osc.sine in:V/Oct
wait connect seq.step Pitch -> osc.sine V/Oct

say And its **Gate** plays each step: patch it into the envelope's **Gate**.
point seq.step out:Gate
point mod.adsr in:Gate
show drag seq.step out:Gate to mod.adsr in:Gate
wait connect seq.step Gate -> mod.adsr Gate

say Press **▶ Play**.
wait play

say Eight steps, all on the same note, round and round. Drag a step up or down to change its note. Give a few of them new notes and the line becomes a tune.
point seq.step step:3
show turn seq.step step:3 up
wait param seq.step Step * Pitch changed

say Keep going until you like it. The step lit up is the one playing.
wait listen 4

say The Clock's **BPM** sets the pace. Turn it up past 140 and the line hurries along.
point util.clock knob:Tempo
show turn util.clock knob:Tempo up
wait param util.clock Tempo > 140

say A clock, a sequencer and a voice: the start of every groove. Try the envelope's **Dec**, or the Oscillator's **Wave**, while it plays.
say Next, **Groups** folds modules away into one.
